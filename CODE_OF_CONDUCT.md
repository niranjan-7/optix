# Code of conduct

We want this to be a welcoming place to learn, especially for people new to trading and to programming.

- **Be kind and patient.** Beginners ask basic questions; that is the point of the project.
- **Be respectful.** No harassment, personal attacks, discrimination or unwelcome behaviour.
- **Assume good faith**, and disagree with ideas rather than people.
- **No pressure to trade.** Do not encourage anyone to risk money they cannot afford to lose. Do not post "tips", guaranteed
  returns, signal-selling, referral links or promotions.
- **Protect privacy.** Never share anyone's credentials, account details or personal information, including your own.

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove contributions that break these rules, and may block repeat offenders.
To report a concern, contact the maintainers through the repository's private reporting options.
